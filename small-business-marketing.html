Small Business Marketing

A client project translating Pancho’s del Lago’s community-focused brand into promotional content.

My contribution

Turned Pancho’s del Lago’s community-focused mission into a clear promotional concept.

As the market researcher, I grounded the creative in the restaurant’s mission and vision. As the client A/E communicator, I translated its business goals into marketing deliverables, kept the client and project team aligned, and helped shape a website-ready ad highlighting live TV, game nights, events, and the restaurant’s role as a gathering place.

What I studied

How the brand could express its mission of bringing different kinds of people together while promoting the experiences that make the restaurant distinctive.

Results

Our team created a promotional poster for the website that connected the restaurant’s food and entertainment offerings with its broader community-focused positioning.

Value to a marketing team

Shows brand research, client communication, message strategy, creative briefing, and the ability to turn business goals into aligned marketing deliverables.

Creative work

One Visual Identity Across Digital Channels.

The project combined advertising design with original photo and video content for practical use by the restaurant.
