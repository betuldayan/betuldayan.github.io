Small Business Marketing

A team project creating promotional content for Pancho's del Lago, a Mexican restaurant.

My contribution

Connected client needs with market research and creative execution.

As the market researcher and client account liaison on a four-person team, I communicated with Pancho’s del Lago, helped shape the promotional direction, and supported photography and videography.

What I studied

How a local restaurant could present its brand more consistently across its website and social media channels.

Results

The team delivered a reusable promotional ad and original visual content designed for the restaurant’s digital channels.

Value to a marketing team

Demonstrates client communication, collaborative planning, content production, and the ability to turn business needs into practical marketing assets.

Creative work

One Visual Identity Across Digital Channels.

The project combined advertising design with original photo and video content for practical use by the restaurant.
