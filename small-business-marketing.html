Small Business Marketing

A client project translating Pancho’s del Lago’s community-focused brand into promotional content.

My contribution

Turned Pancho’s del Lago’s community-focused mission into a clear promotional concept.

As the market researcher, I grounded the creative in the restaurant’s mission and vision. As the client A/E communicator, I translated its business goals into marketing deliverables, kept the client and project team aligned, and helped shape a website-ready ad highlighting live TV, game nights, events, and the restaurant’s role as a gathering place. I also photographed and filmed content for the restaurant, helping carry that strategy into an Instagram video ad.

What I studied

How the brand could express its mission of bringing different kinds of people together while promoting the experiences that make the restaurant distinctive.

Results

The work produced a website-ready promotional poster and an Instagram video ad that connected the restaurant’s food, entertainment, and community-focused positioning.

Value to a marketing team

Shows brand research, client communication, message strategy, creative briefing, and the ability to turn business goals into aligned marketing deliverables.
